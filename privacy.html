RunGroupie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

RunGroupie ("the app") is a training hub for run-walk groups. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to get it removed.

The app is operated by the developer of RunGroupie, contactable at cosmo131@zoho.com.

What the app stores

Your account. When you create an account you give an email address and a password. The password is stored only as a salted hash by our hosting provider; nobody, including us, can read it back.

Your profile. A display name, and optionally a phone number and a contact email you choose to share with your group. Your login email is never shown to other members automatically — only the contact details you deliberately enter. Your profile also holds your running defaults: pace group, run/walk ratio, and your magic mile time.

Your training. Plans and workouts you create, import, or copy from a group template; races you add and any goal times you set; and whether you have marked a workout complete.

Your group activity. Which groups you belong to and your role in them, your RSVPs to weekly run events, and messages you post in an event's chat. Anything you post in a group chat is visible to the other members of that group.

Files you upload. Route description documents uploaded by group leaders are stored so members can open them. Schedules you import by pasting text are parsed on your device; only the resulting workouts are saved.

What the app does not collect

The app does not request or collect your device's location. It has no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or call logs. On Android the app requests only internet access. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share your data with data brokers, and no data is used for advertising or profiling.

Group meeting points and route start points are stored as coordinates, but these describe places, chosen by a group leader — they are not a record of where you have been.

Who else receives data

Supabase hosts the app's database, authentication, and file storage. Data is held there on our behalf as our processor.

Open-Meteo provides the weather line shown on an upcoming run. The app sends it the meeting point's coordinates and the date of the run. It receives nothing that identifies you.

We disclose data otherwise only where the law requires it.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your profile, your plans and workouts, your RSVPs, and your chat messages. Group records created by a leader, such as a route or a scheduled run, remain with the group.

Your choices

You can view and change your profile, contact details, and running defaults at any time from the Profile tab, and delete your own chat messages from the event they were posted in.

To delete your account and the data attached to it, email cosmo131@zoho.com from the address on the account and it will be deleted within 30 days. In-app account deletion is being added.

Children

RunGroupie is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell members in the app.
