RunGroupie Support

RunGroupie keeps your running group's training schedule, group runs, races and chat in one place.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email cosmo131@zoho.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I join my group?

Ask your group leader to invite the email address you use for RunGroupie. Once you sign up with that address, you'll be added to the group automatically.

How do I RSVP for a group run?

Open the Group tab and tap I'm in, Maybe or Out on this week's run.

How do I delete my account?

Email cosmo131@zoho.com from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and its data.
